# Status reporting

A short, regular summary of progress, risks and asks, aimed at the people who need to act on it.

## Why it matters

Good status reporting turns into decisions and help. Bad status reporting turns into a green dashboard right up until the launch slips.

## Pitfalls

- "Watermelon" status: green on the outside, red inside.
- Reporting activity instead of progress against milestones.
- Burying the ask at the bottom of a long update.

## Reflect

- When did you change a status to red, and what happened next?
- How did you structure updates for different audiences?
