# Release management

Planning and running the steps that take finished work safely into production and to users.

## Why it matters

The release is where many teams' work meets real users at once. Clear go/no-go criteria, rollback plans and ownership reduce the blast radius when something goes wrong.

## Pitfalls

- No agreed go/no-go criteria, so the decision becomes a debate on the day.
- A rollback plan that has never been tested.
- Launching everything at once instead of staging or using feature flags.
