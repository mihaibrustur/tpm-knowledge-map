# Shift-left quality

Bringing testing thinking into design and discovery, so quality problems are found before code is written rather than in regression.

## Why it matters

Late QA turns into late releases. When testers help shape the work, testability gaps and edge cases surface while they are cheap to fix.

## Pitfalls

- Inviting QA to discovery meetings without giving them a real voice.
- Measuring QA by bugs found late rather than bugs prevented.
- No shared definition of done, so "implemented" and "shippable" get confused.
