# AI in delivery management

Using language models and agents to draft status, release notes and risk signals, and to join tickets with code changes.

## Why it matters

A lot of program management is collecting and summarizing information. Automation can take that load off, but only if the inputs are reliable and the output is checked.

## Pitfalls

- Generated reports that sound confident on top of incomplete data.
- Automating a broken process instead of fixing it.
- No clear owner for verifying what the tool produces.

- Where can AI agents help delivery management without fabricating confidence?
