# CI/CD and deployment

Automated build, test and deployment pipelines that let teams ship small changes often and safely.

## Why it matters

Pipeline health directly shapes how fast and how safely a program can deliver. Slow or flaky pipelines are a hidden schedule risk.

## Pitfalls

- Ignoring flaky tests until nobody trusts the pipeline.
- Manual deployment steps that only one person knows.
- Big-bang releases instead of small, frequent ones.
