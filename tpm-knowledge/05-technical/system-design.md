# System design basics

How services, data stores, APIs and queues fit together, and the trade-offs between common patterns.

## Why it matters

You do not need to design the system, but you need to follow the discussion, ask good questions and see where design choices create schedule or risk.

## Pitfalls

- Staying silent in design reviews because the topic feels too technical.
- Pretending to understand instead of asking a clarifying question.
- Missing that a design choice creates a new cross-team dependency.
