# Compliance-constrained delivery

Running programs where a security or regulatory standard (for example, card payment security) sets hard gates on scope and timing.

## Why it matters

Compliance work has external assessors, fixed evidence requirements and little room to cut corners. Planning has to treat audits and certifications as critical-path milestones.

## Pitfalls

- Treating compliance as a checklist at the end instead of a design input.
- Underestimating the time external parties take to review and approve.
- Unclear ownership of each control, so evidence is missing at audit time.
