# Mobile release management

Shipping through app stores: branch cuts, store review, staged rollouts and users who update at their own pace.

## Why it matters

Unlike a web service, a mobile release cannot be rolled back instantly, and old versions stay in use. That changes how you think about cadence, risk and what "deployed" means.

## Pitfalls

- Equating "submitted to the store" with "in users' hands".
- Skipping staged rollout for a risky change.
- Applying web-style deployment metrics without adapting them.
