# Non-functional requirements

Qualities like performance, reliability, security, scalability and accessibility, not just features.

## Why it matters

They are often discovered late, during load tests or security reviews, where they become the reason a launch slips.

## Pitfalls

- Not writing them down until a review blocks the launch.
- Targets with no way to measure them.
- Scheduling security and privacy reviews at the very end.
