# Incident management

Responding to production problems with clear roles, communication and follow-up reviews.

## Why it matters

Calm, structured incident response limits user impact, and blameless reviews turn incidents into improvements.

## Pitfalls

- No clear incident lead, so everyone debugs and nobody communicates.
- Post-incident reviews that assign blame.
- Action items from reviews that are never completed.

## Reflect

- What was your role in an incident, and what changed afterwards?
