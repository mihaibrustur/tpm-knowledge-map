# Cross-team coordination

Aligning several teams on shared plans, interfaces and timing so their work fits together.

## Why it matters

The integration points between teams are where work falls through the cracks. Someone has to own the whole picture.

## Pitfalls

- Coordinating only through tickets, without shared conversations.
- Each team optimizing its own plan at the expense of the program.
- Late integration, so mismatches are found at the end.

## Reflect

- How did you keep several teams' plans in sync?
