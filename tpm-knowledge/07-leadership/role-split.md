# Product and delivery roles

The product owner owns what and why; the delivery or program manager owns how work flows: blockers, risks, readiness and predictability.

## Why it matters

When both roles try to own priorities, or nobody owns flow, teams get mixed signals. A clear split lets each role be decisive in its own lane.

## Pitfalls

- The delivery role drifting into deciding priorities.
- The product role absorbing all coordination and burning out.
- Agreeing the split once and never revisiting it as the team grows.
