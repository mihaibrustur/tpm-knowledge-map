# Retrospectives and learning

Regularly looking back at what worked and what did not, and turning that into concrete changes.

## Why it matters

A program that learns gets easier over time. Without follow-through, retrospectives become a ritual that people stop believing in.

## Pitfalls

- The same issues raised every time with no change.
- Action items with no owner.
- An atmosphere where people do not feel safe being honest.
