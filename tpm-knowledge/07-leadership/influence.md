# Influence without authority

Getting people who do not report to you to commit, through shared goals, trust and clear reasoning.

## Why it matters

It is the core of the TPM role. Most of the people a program depends on have their own managers and priorities.

## Pitfalls

- Relying on borrowed authority ("the VP wants this") too often.
- Asking for help without explaining why it matters to them.
- Building relationships only when you need something.

## Reflect

- How did you get a team with other priorities to commit to your work?
