# Conflict resolution

Helping people with different goals or views reach a decision they can both commit to.

## Why it matters

Unresolved conflict quietly slows programs down. Surfacing it and focusing on shared goals keeps work moving.

## Pitfalls

- Avoiding conflict until it becomes a crisis.
- Taking sides instead of clarifying the trade-off.
- Treating a disagreement about facts as a disagreement about people.
