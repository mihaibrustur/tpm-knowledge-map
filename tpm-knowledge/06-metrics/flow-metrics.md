# Flow metrics

Cycle time, throughput and work in progress, which describe how work moves through a team.

## Why it matters

They give an evidence-based basis for forecasts and show where work waits, which is usually more than where it is worked on.

## Pitfalls

- Too much work in progress, which slows everything down.
- Forecasting from story points alone instead of actual throughput.
- Looking at averages and missing the long tail of slow items.

## Reflect

- Where did work wait the longest in a team you supported?
