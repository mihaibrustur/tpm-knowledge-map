# Leading and lagging indicators

Lagging indicators confirm a result after the fact; leading indicators predict it early enough to act.

## Why it matters

Steering a program only on lagging indicators means you find out too late. Pairing them gives both early warning and confirmation.

## Pitfalls

- Tracking only what is easy to measure.
- Leading indicators that do not actually predict the outcome.
- Too many metrics, so none of them drive decisions.
