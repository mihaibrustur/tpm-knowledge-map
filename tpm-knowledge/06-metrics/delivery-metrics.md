# Delivery metrics (DORA)

Deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate and time to restore service.

## Why it matters

They give a balanced view of speed and stability, so teams do not improve one at the expense of the other.

## Pitfalls

- Using them to compare or rank teams.
- Chasing the number instead of the underlying practice.
- Measuring speed without stability, or the reverse.
