# Milestones and critical path

Breaking the program into checkpoints and finding the chain of dependent work that sets the earliest finish date.

## Why it matters

Only delays on the critical path move the end date. Knowing which work is on it tells you where to focus attention and where slack exists.

## Pitfalls

- Milestones defined as activities ("start testing") rather than verifiable results.
- Forgetting that the critical path shifts as work finishes or slips.
- Ignoring near-critical paths that have very little slack.
