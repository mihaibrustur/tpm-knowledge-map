# Intake and prioritization

A single, visible way for requests to enter the team, get triaged and be ranked against the work already planned.

## Why it matters

Without one front door, requests arrive through side channels and the loudest one wins. A clear intake with response targets protects the plan and shows requesters where they stand.

## Pitfalls

- Several back doors (direct messages, hallway asks) that bypass the process.
- Ranking that happens in one person's head.
- Treating expedite, maintenance and roadmap work as one undifferentiated queue.

## Reflect

- How would you set up intake and prioritization with a single product owner?
- How did you handle a request that jumped the queue?
