# Dependency mapping

Identifying which teams, systems or decisions your program relies on, and who relies on you.

## Why it matters

Cross-team dependencies are where programs most often stall, because no single team owns the gap between them.

## Pitfalls

- Recording a dependency without an owner and a needed-by date on the other side.
- Assuming the other team has the same priority for your request.
- Only mapping inbound dependencies and forgetting who is waiting on you.
