# Discovery and readiness

Shaping work one cycle ahead with product, design, engineering and QA, so it is understood before anyone commits to building it.

## Why it matters

Most mid-sprint surprises are questions nobody asked before the sprint started. A short, shared readiness check catches them while changing course is still cheap.

## Pitfalls

- Readiness criteria so heavy that nothing is ever ready.
- Discovery done by product alone, with engineering and QA seeing it at planning.
- Confusing a finished design with a buildable, testable item.
