# Scope definition

Writing down what the program will and will not deliver, and the outcome it is meant to achieve.

## Why it matters

Most late programs are not slow, they are bigger than anyone agreed to. A clear scope, including explicit non-goals, gives you something to point at when new requests arrive.

## Pitfalls

- Listing features without the outcome they serve, so nobody can judge trade-offs.
- Skipping non-goals, which leaves room for silent scope creep.
- Treating the first scope as fixed instead of revisiting it as you learn.

## Reflect

- When did unclear scope cause a program to drift?
- How did you get agreement on what was out of scope?
