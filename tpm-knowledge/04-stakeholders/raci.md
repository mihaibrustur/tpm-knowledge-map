# RACI and ownership

Making clear who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed for each key decision or deliverable.

## Why it matters

Unclear ownership causes both gaps (nobody does it) and conflicts (two people think they decide). One accountable person per item avoids both.

## Pitfalls

- More than one Accountable for the same item.
- A detailed chart nobody reads; the value is in the conversation.
- Consulting so many people that decisions stall.
