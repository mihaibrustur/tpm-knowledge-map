# Executive communication

Communicating with senior leaders: bottom line first, short, and clear about what you need from them.

## Why it matters

Executives have little time and many inputs. If the decision or ask is not obvious in the first lines, it will likely be missed.

## Pitfalls

- Leading with background instead of the conclusion.
- Hiding bad news in the details.
- Presenting a problem without options or a recommendation.

- How did you deliver bad news to leadership?
- What format worked best for a busy executive?
