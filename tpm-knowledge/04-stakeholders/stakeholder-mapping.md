# Stakeholder mapping

Listing who is affected by or has influence over the program, and what each of them needs.

## Why it matters

Programs fail politically as often as technically. Knowing who to involve, inform or win over prevents late surprises.

## Pitfalls

- Only mapping the people in the room, missing quiet but powerful stakeholders.
- Treating all stakeholders the same way.
- Not revisiting the map when the organization changes.

## Reflect

- Which stakeholder did you underestimate, and what did you learn?
