# Risk register

A living list of things that might go wrong, with likelihood, impact, an owner and a response.

## Why it matters

Writing risks down with owners turns vague worry into actions. Reviewing it regularly keeps attention on what could hurt the program most.

## Pitfalls

- A register created at kickoff and never opened again.
- Risks with no owner or no response, just a description.
- Confusing risks (might happen) with issues (already happening).

## Reflect

- Which risk on your list actually happened, and were you ready?
- How often did you review risks, and with whom?
