# Pre-mortem

Imagining the program has failed and working backwards to list the most likely reasons.

## Why it matters

It gives people permission to voice doubts they would otherwise keep to themselves, and it fills the risk register with real concerns.

## Pitfalls

- Running it once and not turning the findings into owned risks.
- Letting the most senior voice dominate the list.
- Only listing technical failures and missing people or process ones.

## Reflect

- What did a pre-mortem surface that nobody had said out loud?
