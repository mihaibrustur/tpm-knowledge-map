# Escalation

Bringing a problem to people with more authority when the team cannot resolve it within its own scope.

## Why it matters

Escalating early and clearly is a tool, not a failure. It gets decisions made while there are still options.

## Pitfalls

- Escalating too late, after the options have run out.
- Escalating a complaint instead of a decision with clear options.
- Surprising peers by escalating without telling them first.

- How did you keep the relationship with the other team intact?
