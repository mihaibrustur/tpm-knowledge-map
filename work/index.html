A cybersecurity scale-up · Program manager

Coordinating execution across a scaling R&D organization

Challenge Keeping delivery predictable while engineering grew to about a hundred people, as the only program manager and with no authority over the teams.

Outcome Every team on one cadence within about two months, then a move from a gated process to shared principles, cross-functional pods and engineering health signals as the organization grew.
