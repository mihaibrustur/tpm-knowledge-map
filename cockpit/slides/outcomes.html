What you get

Four things leaders notice first

Dates you can trust

Forecast ranges based on what teams actually deliver, not on estimates made up front.

Early warnings

A heads-up when work stalls, scope grows or quality slips, while there is still time to act.

Less status work

Weekly updates and release notes drafted for you, ready for a person to check.

Better conversations

Meetings move from “when?” to priorities and trade-offs.

These are the benefits, not the features. If asked how forecasts work: they use each team’s real delivery history and give a range with a confidence level, not a single date.
