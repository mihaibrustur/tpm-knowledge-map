Measures and warnings

What we check at each step

StepWhat we checkExample warning
RaisedCases per week by type, and the share escalated to engineeringEscalations doubled after the last release
EscalatedTime from case to Jira ticket; tickets missing steps to reproduceCases wait days in support before engineering sees them
TriagedTime to triage; how many “bugs” turn out to be configuration or usageHalf of this month’s bugs were closed as configuration
BuiltCycle time, review wait, work in progress, pull requests linked to ticketsFixes are waiting days for a first review
ReleasedTime from merge to release; fixes that reopen or cause regressionsThe same issue reopened after the release
ClosedTime from release to customer update; total time from report to fixA fix shipped, but the customer wasn’t told for a week
Each warning goes to one owner in Slack, with the cases and tickets behind it. Thresholds are set against each team’s own normal during the first weeks of the pilot, not against a generic target.
