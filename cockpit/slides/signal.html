Review queue

Example

Nine pull requests have waited more than two days for a first review

Usual for this team

Two

Likely cause

Two reviewers moved to release work on Monday

Question

Who can take reviews this week?

Sent to

Team channel, engineering manager

Specific

It shows the exact items behind the warning.

Explained

It offers a likely cause, clearly labelled as a guess.

Owned

It goes to one person, in the channel they already use.

A question, not an order

The team decides what to do, including nothing.

The example is invented, but it is a real pattern: in one organisation, pull requests regularly waited too long for review because people lost track of them. Simple alerts fixed it and the problem did not come back.
