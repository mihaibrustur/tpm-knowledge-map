Getting started

Start small: one pilot, about two months

Weeks 1–2

Connect

Read-only access, a light data clean-up, and each team’s normal measured.

Weeks 3–8

Run

Weekly warnings and forecasts for one or two teams, reviewed together.

Week 9

Decide

Look at what was acted on and what changed. Expand, adjust or stop.

Low risk: nothing changes in how teams work until the pilot shows it is worth it.

The timeline is a proposal and can flex. The decision point at the end is the important part: the pilot has to earn its expansion.
