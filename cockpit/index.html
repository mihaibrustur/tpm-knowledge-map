The idea

One shared view of how engineering delivers, and an assistant that tells you where to look.

It runs on the tools your teams already use. No new process to learn, no extra reporting.

What you get

Four things leaders notice first

Dates you can trust

Forecast ranges based on what teams actually deliver, not on estimates made up front.

Early warnings

A heads-up when work stalls, scope grows or quality slips, while there is still time to act.

Less status work

Weekly updates and release notes drafted for you, ready for a person to check.

Better conversations

Meetings move from “when?” to priorities and trade-offs.

Getting started

Start small: one pilot, about two months

Weeks 1–2

Connect

Read-only access, a light data clean-up, and each team’s normal measured.

Weeks 3–8

Run

Weekly warnings and forecasts for one or two teams, reviewed together.

Week 9

Decide

Look at what was acted on and what changed. Expand, adjust or stop.

Low risk: nothing changes in how teams work until the pilot shows it is worth it.
